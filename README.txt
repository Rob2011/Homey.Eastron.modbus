'Eastron' brings reliable energy measurements from  DIN Rail Energy Meters over Modbus TCP directly into Homey Pro. Whether you want to monitor total power consumption, per-phase values, or energy production insights, this app delivers real-time data to your Homey Energy dashboard and smart flows.

Build smart flows based on live energy data, visualize consumption within the Homey Energy dashboard and optimize your energy management. Turn your Energy Meter with Modbus TCP gateway into a powerful part of your smart home. Supported meters: Eastron SDM120 / SDM120CT, SDM220, SDM230, SDM72D-M-2, SDM630 (V2/V3), SDM630MCT (incl. 40mA) and SDM630MCT-RC (Rogowski coil). Meters with an RS485 interface need a Modbus TCP gateway; meters with built-in Modbus TCP (such as the SDM630-TCP) can be connected directly.
